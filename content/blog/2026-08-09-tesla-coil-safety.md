# Tesla coil safety, revisited

Standard operating distance from the coil terminals is two meters, not "as
close as looks good in the photo." We're adding a floor marking.

Capacitor banks stay charged longer than you'd think even after shutdown --
always verify with the meter before touching anything downstream of the
spark gap.
