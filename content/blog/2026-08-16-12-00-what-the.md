# WHAT THE...?

I've created this website for the pedestrian reason of linking to the company's
socials, such as they are, including nuget.org, which is included for irony.
Hey Bob, did you check out my latest post on nuget.org?  It's a banger are
words that live in infamy, even as they're said.

Furthermore, the company has adopted YouTube to track progress on our homely
project, so this site is a natural place to link to those videos and house
longer, more technical descriptions.  For an audience of one (not _that_ one).

It should be noted: this is not a design operation.  Management has limited
skills in creative styling in web design, so this is really minimalist with
some intentinoally awful choices.

This website was developed by claude code with particular requirements about
the technology stack and the mechanism by which it renders content. 
Effectively, though I wish I could have developed the site myself, owing to the
vast and sprawling nature of my main project there just aren't enough hours in
the day.

This website represents everything I would have done had I had the ability
working as a web developer.  The fact I couldn't do such things is what lead me
towards infrastructure over web development.

This website is SEO poor.  That is a deliberate choice I made.  It's not a
typical choice, and not a choice most businesses could abide.  My choice for
my website was to eschew SEO and nosey agents.
