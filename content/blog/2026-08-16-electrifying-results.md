# SYNOPSIS

We ran twelve thousand volts through a pie. Here's what we learned, and why
the smoke detector budget for next quarter just tripled.

# Electrifying results

The pie held together longer than expected -- structural integrity of the
crust seems to improve slightly under sustained arc exposure, possibly from
localized moisture flash-boiling and re-setting the gluten network near the
surface. We don't have a rigorous explanation yet, just a lot of very good
photos.

Next steps: repeat with a laminated dough, and get the meter technician to
stop standing quite so close.
