# Embuscade

![Embuscade](./Embuscade.png)

_Embuscade_ is the French word for wasting time.  I've been thinking about
browser games since first learning about websockets many years ago.  I never did
anything about it because I just really got sick of web development.  For one
thing, I've never worked anywhere that I could even mention websockets.  Every
manager I've ever had has enough technical knowledge to say no, well, to pretty
much everything that isn't PII-harvesting technology.

With Claude now I can delegate the web work, so that's what I did.  This little
project is heavily inspired by a classic 80s game - Bolo.  And about 15,000
little knockoff tank-shooter games.  The adroit amongst you may recognize the
game creation and matching feature.  This was heavily inspired by bungie'net's
Myth: TFL and Myth II: Soulblighter online gameplay.  There's no ranked, and
will be strictly over my dead body!! or something.

You don't see many projects like this - they're mainly in this context - little
one-off demos meant to burnish a portfolio.  And what do you know, that's more
or less what this is.  It's truly a shame, because this is interesting
technology and could be put to interesting uses.  I think the reason you don't
see this more:

* it's harder to monetise than grabbing Android Studio or Xamarin and cranking
  out another little advertising driven or pay to play knockoff.
* not everyone appreciates keyboard only games.  Moreover, if you start out
  writing a little game like this your first thought has to be mobile: you want
  people playing on their phones while they're on the bus, or in line at
  starbucks, or, apparently, in the left turn lane ahead of me when the light
  turns green.
* web 2.0 has been a pretty cynical cash grab in my estimation at least.  Most
  shops don't want interesting websites, they want websites that harvest email
  addresses that can be targetted.  They want websites that maximise advertising
  impressions.

Be that as it may, I'm proud of my little portfolio-burnisher.  Join with the
tank icon in the top rail: pop online and try your luck against me.  For the
bold at heart: use the chat feature to challenge me and I'll pop on.  You have
to be really bold for that, though.
